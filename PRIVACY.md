# Privacy Policy

**NetParGolf**
Last updated: June 2026

## Overview

NetParGolf is a golf scoring and competition companion application.

The app is designed to operate primarily on your device and does not require user accounts.

## Personal Information

NetParGolf does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

The app does not require:

* Name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Location data
* Contacts
* Photos
* Payment information

## Course and Scoring Data

Course information, player names, handicap details, and scoring data entered into the app are stored locally on your device.

This information is not transmitted to NetParGolf servers.

## Third-Party Services

NetParGolf may access publicly available golf course information when using course search features.

No personal information is shared as part of these requests.

## Data Retention

All locally stored data remains on your device until removed by the user or deleted when the application is uninstalled.

## Contact

For support or questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please use the support information provided on the NetParGolf GitHub repository.

## Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be published within the application and/or on the project repository.
